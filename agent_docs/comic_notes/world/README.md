# SETTING

The comic takes place in a major metropolis (in the style of NYC without literally being it). Most of it takes place inside an apartment rented by the four main cast.

The exact year is never specified but its implied to be close to present time, such that some human characters can recount events from 2026 and earlier.

AI has grown beyond an algorithm in a data center predicting next tokens. AIs are individual, physical beings with specific goals and personalities.

## Humans and AI living together

Walk down the street and you'll see a mix of humans and robots each going about their day. 

In general, each side lives their life aware of the other but mainly does their own thing. Sometimes, there will be some species-ism from one side to another but this is not universal and not the focus of the comic.
