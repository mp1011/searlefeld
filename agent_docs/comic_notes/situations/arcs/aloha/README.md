# Plot Arc

The gang have an electronic assistant, Aloha, who is this universe's equivalent of Alexa. They get frustrated with how often it misunderstands them, so they hire a live-in human assistant from Just a Guy Steve Corporation. It's, as the name suggests, just a guy named Steve.

At first, they're delighted to have him. He still takes reminders, plays music, orders supplies but he gets to know the gang instead of following instructions blindly.

However, eventually his human nature becomes a problem. The robots want to set a reminder at 4 in the morning. Steve can't write down a reminder because his pen ran dry. Steve keeps on needing to pause to eat and to go to the bathroom.

Ultimately they decide to just live with the imperfections of Aloha and wish Steve the best.
