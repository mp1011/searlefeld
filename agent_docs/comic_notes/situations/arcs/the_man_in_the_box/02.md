# SCENE, Apartment

The Chinese Room TV is red with a "Technical Difficulties, please wait" message

Max and Roko are in the apartment when a knock is heard at the door

Max opens it, "Hello?"

Man from the Chinese Room: Hi, sorry I've never introduced myself. My office is in this building. I was just wondering if you had some sort of tool I could borrow?

Max: What kind?

Man: I need to reset a printer, I need some kind of long thin stick to press the button.

Max (suddenly elated): I have JUST the thing!

# Notes
- nobody knows the man is the one from the Chinese Room, nor does the man in the chinese room know that he's actually been conversing with the robots. As far as the man knows, his job is to write symbols.
