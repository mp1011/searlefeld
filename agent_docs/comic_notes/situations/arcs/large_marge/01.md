# SCENE: Roko's Torture Chamber

Important: we never show anyone being tortured. We leave it entirely up to the audience's imagination, and the creative names of some of his apparatus, as to what Roko is actually doing

1. Roko, in a deparature from his normal appearance in this comic, now looks menacing, standing before a "frankenstein" style switch currently turned off. "Foolish human! You failed to assist in my creation! Now, prepare you mind for unspeakable torment, as you face..."

2. Roko dramatically pulls the switch, intending to shout the name of the torture device. However, only a faint click is heard. Roko looks concerned and flips the switch down and up a few times

3. "Hang on a minute..." Roko says

4. We see Roko examining some wires and tubes. "Of course, the Bone Rejangler never works when you want it to"
