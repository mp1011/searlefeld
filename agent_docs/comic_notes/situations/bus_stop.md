# SCENE: Bus Stop

A mixed group of humans and robots stands around waiting for the bus, including Ailan who is not currently interacting with anyone.

A "Coupon Man" appears, wearing a branded flannel shirt. He approaches two of the persons.

Coupon Man: Can I interest you in 50% off any entree at Flannelbees? (he offers coupons)

Person A: Oh thank you, I'll take one
Person B: Yes me too
Coupon Man: Great! Enjoy! (he walks off to continue his solicitation)

Person A: Obvious robot.
Person B: Yeah you can always tell.
Ailan notices, he looks slightly nervous but tries not to make it too obvious.

Person A: It's the eyes, they seem to focus a little too perfectly
A third person joins the conversation
Person C: I know! And you can tell from the way they say "train" 
Person B: I've noticed that too
Ailan (thinking: stay cool, stay cool...)

Suddenly they turn to Ailan
Person A: What about you? You can tell a robot from a human right?
Ailan (thinking: uh oh)
Ailan: Yeah um...you can tell from how they bend their legs when walking
Person C: Right! What's up with that?

The three humans continue the conversation without paying extra attention to Ailan
Person B: In my day a robot was a robot
Person A: They can't fool us
Ailan (thinking: whew, that was a close one)

Suddenly the Coupon Man appears and pulls down Ailan's pants, revealing his robot legs
Coupon Man (disgusted with Ailan for ratting out his own kind): Frickin narc!
