Ailan is enjoying time with a diverse group of humans, who are in the middle of a conversation

Person 1: So then he walks in with a three foot Italian hero!

Person 2: My man Ailan.
Close up on Ailan, emotionally crushed: "Oh no, I'm a total fraud..."
