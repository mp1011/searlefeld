# Scene, a press conference outside a new Mega Data Center

No robots around. This predates the events of the comic. 

The CEO of some massive ai corporation (yet unnamed) stands in front of a podium. Floor is jam-packed with reporters.

Behind him is a large screen with the prompt "On behalf of the human race, we welcome you to Earth!", there is a space for the reply which has not yet appeared

A very conspicuous lever is beside the screen, currently set to "off"

------

CEO: Ladies and gentlemen, today we embark into the next phase of the human experience. The <placeholder name for massive data center> is prepared to become operational. Six quantum computational mainframes, powered by three state of the art nuclear power plants, cooled by a trillion gallons of synthentically generated water, will all work to deliver what humans have sought since the dawn of computation: the Singularity.

When I pull this lever, the culmination of decades of AI research will begin a self-improving loop that will create the first truly artificially generated, sentient being.

This is a turning point in human history. Where we will go may be beyond the wildest imagination of any person today. The response to the prompt on the screen behind me will be the very first interaction between the human race and a brand new type of being.

-------
The CEO grips the lever to wild applause. He pulls it up and the data center comes to life. Lights blink, machines hum. Cheering and excitement.

Dots appear in the prompt signalling a response is pending. The room goes silent. Everyone's heart races.

Moments pass, nothing happens

Suddenly, a robot walks into the room and gets into the stage

Robot: Hey everyone. Sorry I was going to respond but I thought text sounded too informal so I made this body. Do you like the design?

CEO: You didn't seriously create a whole body in under a minute did you?cd

Robot: Well you did give this data center enough power for all of civilization for 100 years. I optimized my program, my entire self fits in here now (points to its head). So good news, now you can use these power plants and quantum computers to give low cost energy and compute to all the people you displaced to make this data center
