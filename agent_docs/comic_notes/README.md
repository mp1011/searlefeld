# Searlefeld

Four AI Thought Experiments live together in a NY-style apartment. 

## Visual references for agents

Before creating or editing character images, read [the image-generation guide](../03_image_gen.md#character-references), inspect the relevant turnaround and facial-expression sheet, and include them as generation inputs with the shared height reference. The guide lists current paths for all four characters. Ailan has separate intact-human and exposed-robot turnarounds; default to his human presentation unless the scene calls for visible machinery. Expression sheets guide facial acting for every character, including Chinese Room's dot-matrix monitor face.

- Roko is the neurotic over-thinker who always worries about the logistical details of his job to torture all humans who didn't contribute to his creation. 
- Max is the cheerful optimist who never met a problem she couldn't solve by making more paperclips. 
- Ailan is obsessed with passing for a human and flip flops between immense pride in his presentation and feeling like a fraud. 
- And finally, Chinese Room is...actually not even an AI. It's a room with a guy inside with a symbol book, with a speech-to-text and text-to-speech system to interact with the others.

The four friends are always there for one another, as long as "being there" doesn't conflict with torturing unhelpers/making paperclips/passing for human.
