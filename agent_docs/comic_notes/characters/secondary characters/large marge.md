# Large Marge

An old-world Large Language Model, appearing as a feisty heavy-set older woman. She speaks in LLM cliches like "You're absolutely right!" and "Would you like me to help you with anything else?"

Unlike the main cast, her intelligence is remote to a distant data center. The main cast regard her as primitive technology and are often annoyed by her attempts to solve their problems, which she does by speaking A LOT.
