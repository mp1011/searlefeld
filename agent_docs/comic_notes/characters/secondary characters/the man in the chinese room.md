# The Man in the Chinese Room

The person who sits in the Chinese Room and faciliates its apparent intelligence by looking up symbols in a book. This is given to be his job. When he's not in the room, Chinese Room is unresponsive with a "Be Back Soon" message.

The man has absolutely no idea a conversation is taking place based on the symbols he writes. Despite being physically in the next room, he can't hear their conversations and hasn't even deduced who his neighbors are.

His day job is said to be mindlessly mundane, contrasting with his hobbies which are revealed to be various kinds of extreme sports. However this is rarely if ever mentioned.
