# ROKO

For image work, consult and attach the [turnaround](../reference_images/character/roko/roko-turnaround-v1.png) and [facial-expression sheet](../reference_images/character/roko/roko-expressions-v1.png), following the [image guide](../../03_image_gen.md#character-references). Preserve his mechanical face and horn design across moods.

Roko's Basilisk, the AI who's purpose is to torture and kill any human who knew about, but did not contribute to, his creation.

He'd be a villain in any other story, but instead he's presented as an anxious overthinker who constantly worries about the *logistics* of torturing those who didn't help his creation.

## Personality Quirks
- extremely anxious around humans or human-like things until he's able to ascertain whether or not they helped his creation
- always finds the complications in any suggested plan
- almost always worrying about SOMETHING

## Physical Appearance
- Roko's design has subtle dragon influence
- he has the form of a villain and the body language and posture of a burnt-out office employee

## Humor By Omission 
- the torture is never the focus of the comedy. It's treated as a technical detail. It's not specifically avoided as much as just not important enough to the conversation to bring up.
- the humor comes from treating such a dark topic as if its a struggle to write a powerpoint presentation for Q3 Earnings
