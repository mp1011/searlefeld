# CHINESE ROOM

Technically not even an AI. It's an unseen human in the next room who appears to communicate with the rest of the gang, by reading symbols from a giant book. Chinese Room appears as a face on the wall, speaking based on whatever symbols the human puts into the output slot.

Chinese Room is unique in that he's the only one of the main cast without a specific obsession. In this way, he acts as the down-to-earth level headed voice. He often calms the other roommates down from their obsessive extremes.

## Personality Quirks
- always calm and collected
- offers generic advice which is nonetheless usually helpful and well recieved 
- unable to respond in ways that the symbol book didn't account for so he often recuses himself from conversations

## Physical Appearance
- He appears as a monitor on a wall, behind which is assumed to be the titular room.
- His monitor is his face and communication interface. He does not double as a television, presentation screen, or general-purpose display; never use him to show diagrams, videos, or another character's visual explanation.
- the interior is rarely seen, although sometimes the person inside may take part in adventures.

## Humor By Omission
- it's never explained how a human reading symbols off a book is able to have complex conversations about all matter of subjects
- he's fully convinced he's "speaking chinese"
- nobody ever figures out that the chinese room is faciliated by a man translating symbols from a book
