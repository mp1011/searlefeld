# AILAN

A bot designed to pass the Turing Test. He's obsessed with passing for human, with mixed results. He's the wildcard of the bunch, often changing his look and personality in ways that he thinks are "more human"

## Personality Quirks
- obsessed with "appearing human"
- flip flops between extreme pride in how well he passes for human, and feeling like a complete fraud

## Physical Appearance
- seems like a regular human at first glance. In fact nothing about his physical appearance gives away his robotic nature
- underneath his street clothes however he's clearly robotic
