FLANNELBEES — RESTAURANT IMAGE REFERENCE

CONCEPT AND WORLD
Flannelbees is an in-universe, family-friendly chain restaurant with a flannel
theme. The feel is warm, approachable, comfortably corny corporate rustic:
an affordable casual family restaurant, rather than a luxury lodge.
It exists in a city setting. Restaurants serve humans because robots do not
eat. Robots can work as cooks or waiters. Show human diners; robots shown
working should be serving, taking orders, or cooking, never eating.
Human staff can also appear.

ESTABLISHED VISUAL DIRECTION
The selected direction is the classic red-plaid lodge look:
- Red-and-black buffalo plaid booth upholstery and staff shirts.
- Honey-colored wood paneling, wooden tables, and substantial timber beams.
- Warm amber lighting and pine-pattern pendant lamps.
- Cozy, slightly kitschy woodland decor and a decorative stone fireplace.
- Frequent stylized pine-tree motifs: lampshades, framed art, booth dividers,
  carved trim, window decals, table accessories, and subtle carpet patterns.
- Casual American family meals, such as burgers and fries, suit the references.
Keep the layout practical for restaurant service and conversations in comics.

CITY EXTERIOR
The restaurant occupies a street-level storefront in an urban building.
Show sidewalks, neighboring storefronts, and multistory city buildings.
Combine warm wood storefront trim with the surrounding urban brick facade,
large inviting windows, red awnings with plaid accents, amber lanterns, and
repeated pine silhouettes. The exterior can display the name "Flannelbees".
Do not turn it into a remote cabin, forest resort, or suburban parking-lot site.

INTERIOR
Do not display the restaurant name inside. Use recurring pine motifs to
communicate the brand instead of interior wordmarks. The approved city
interior reference uses symbol-only decor and accessories.
Street-facing windows should establish the city outside when visible.
Maintain the cozy lodge styling within this urban space.
