Scene: A table at a cafe.

Ailan: So what do human beings like us do with our time?

Man: Oh I've mostly been focusing on my career.

Ailan: Career! Of course! What is career?

Man: I sit in a room for 8 hours a day, pieces of paper get printed with symbols on them, I look up those symbols in a book, write other symbols onto paper and put it back into a slot.

Ailan: That sounds great!

Man: Yeah, it helps pay for my bear wrestling in fire jetski racing.

Ailan: I suppose any hobby has its boring parts.
